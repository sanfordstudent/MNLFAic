# aDIFic 0.0.0.9000

* First version: anchor-free inference for uniform (difficulty) DIF in
  moderated nonlinear factor analysis with person and item covariates
  (`mnlfa_ic()`), with summaries, item-level tables, identification
  diagnostics, a simulator, and the stage functions for advanced use.
* The cluster-robust covariance (`cluster =`) is experimental.
